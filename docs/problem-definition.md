# CivicPulse — Problem Definition

## Problem

Infrastructure problems in local communities can be difficult to report,
track, and follow through until they are resolved.

Problems such as potholes, broken streetlights, water leaks, damaged public
facilities, blocked drains, and illegal dumping may be reported through
different channels or may not be reported at all.

When reports are scattered, it becomes difficult to know:

- What problems have been reported
- Where the problems are located
- Which problems are urgent
- Which problems are currently being addressed
- Which problems have been resolved
- How long a problem has remained unresolved

## Proposed Solution

CivicPulse is a cloud-based infrastructure incident reporting platform.

The platform allows community members to submit infrastructure problems,
provide relevant information about the problem, attach photographic evidence,
and track the progress of their reports.

Administrators can view reported incidents, prioritise them, update their
status, and mark them as resolved or rejected.

## Goal

The goal of CivicPulse is to provide a centralised way to report and manage
community infrastructure problems while giving users visibility into the
progress of their reports.

## Initial Scope

The first version of CivicPulse will support:

- Creating an infrastructure report
- Viewing an individual report
- Viewing multiple reports
- Updating a report
- Tracking report status
- Categorising infrastructure problems
- Setting report priority
- Attaching photographic evidence

The system will initially focus on the core reporting workflow. Additional
features will be introduced only when they provide a clear benefit to the
problem being solved.
